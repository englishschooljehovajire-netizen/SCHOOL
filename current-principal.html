Current Principal - JJES Pangbalkawn

Mr. Timothy Ngente

Mr. Timothy Vanlaltluanga Ngente is the eldest son of our School Founder, Mr. Laltlanthanga Ngente. He is the backbone and the driving force behind the successful management and steady growth of JJES, Pangbalkawn. A man with a profound passion for learning, he holds a Bachelor of Computer Application (B.C.A) in Programming & Systems. Beyond his degree, he is a multi-skilled professional. He is a certified Tally Expert, well-versed in School Administration and Management, Cyber Security, AI Teacher Skills, and has also completed Intermediate in Music.

He is a lifelong learner who constantly strives to acquire new knowledge and skills. Apart from the degrees and certificates mentioned above, he possesses many other professional qualifications that make him a versatile leader. With his unique blend of technical expertise, administrative acumen, and artistic talent, he has brought modern and innovative methods into our school system. His vision is to provide quality education combined with technology and moral values. Under his able leadership, JJES continues to march forward towards excellence.
