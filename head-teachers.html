A Tribute to Our Dedicated Teachers of Jehova Jire English School - JJES Pangbalkawn

Our Head Teachers are not merely administrators; they are the very foundation, the heart, and the soul of our institution. We have proudly displayed them here to publicly honour their immense value, to acknowledge how deeply we cherish their contributions, and to show the world how proud we are to have them as part of our family. The journey of JJES Pangbalkawn from its humble beginnings to what it is today would not have been possible without their visionary leadership and unwavering dedication. They have led with wisdom, served with humility, and taught with passion. Through challenging times and moments of celebration, they have stood as pillars of strength, ensuring that the light of education continues to shine brightly for every child in Pangbalkawn. Their contribution is immeasurable. They have shaped not just the curriculum, but the character of our students. They have mentored our teachers, guided our parents, and built a community bound by the love for learning. Their tireless efforts, late evenings, and selfless sacrifices often go unseen, but their impact is visible in every student who walks out of our school with confidence, knowledge, and values.

We are profoundly grateful for their service. We take immense pride in their leadership, for they represent the best of what education stands for — integrity, compassion, and a relentless pursuit of excellence. They are our mentors, our guides, and our inspiration. To our past and present Head Teachers — you are the pride of JJES Pangbalkawn. Your legacy is etched into the walls of our school and into the hearts of all whose lives you have touched. We honour you, we thank you, and we will forever be indebted to your noble service.

Former Head Teachers Head Teacher (2025 - Present)
