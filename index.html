About Us

JJES Pangbalkawn refers to Jehova Jire English School, an educational institution established in 2011 located in Pangbalkawn village, within the Kolasib district of Mizoram, India. About the School Location: Pangbalkawn, Bilkhawthlir block, Kolasib district, Mizoram (PIN 796081). Estd: 10th Jan. 2011. Motto: "Brighten up your future." Activities: Regularly hosts community and academic events like health awareness programs and many more.
