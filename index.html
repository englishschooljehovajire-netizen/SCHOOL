Jehova Jire English School,
Pangbalkawn

Jehova Jire English School is situated at a prime spot in Pangbalkawn village, 100 metres from the nearby Community Hall, Pangbalkawn, Bilkhawthlir Block, Kolasib, Mizoram - 796081. A branch of JJES, the school's foundation was laid on 10th January 2011, with the motto "Brighten Up Your Future." The school is Govt. Recognised, Co-educational English Medium School.

All subjects are taught in English, Mizo and Hindi. We also teach Music, Public Speaking and Computer. In Computer, we teach only the basics of Programming, System and Microsoft Office. Our school campus is in a quiet place, with clean air, safe drinking water and easy wheelchair access.
