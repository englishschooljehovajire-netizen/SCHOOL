Principal Message

Dear Friends,

It gives me immense joy and pride to welcome you to Jehova Jire English School - JJES, Pangbalkawn.

As the Principal of this institution, established on 10th January 2011 with the Motto "Brighten up your future," I firmly believe that a school is not just a building with classrooms, but a second home where a child's future is nurtured with care, discipline, and love. At JJES, we are deeply committed to creating an inclusive and accessible environment for all. Our campus is designed to promote strong connectivity and inclusiveness. The entire school premises are wheelchair accessible, ensuring that every child, parent, and visitor can move freely and with dignity. We believe that no child should feel left out because of physical barriers.

What makes JJES truly special is our location. Situated in the peaceful village of Pangbalkawn, our students are blessed with a clean, safe, and healthy environment. Far from the chaos of the city, our children breathe fresh air and learn in a calm atmosphere. Here, noise pollution is minimal, which helps our students to concentrate better, think clearly, and learn joyfully. This natural tranquility is a gift that urban schools cannot offer. Though we are located in a village, our standard is never compromised. We maintain high academic and administrative standards at par with any reputed institution. We give the utmost importance to punctuality, cleanliness, and discipline. These are not just rules at JJES; they are values we live by every day. A clean uniform, a punctual arrival, and a respectful attitude form the foundation of our school culture. The safety of our children is our first priority. Our school building is structurally safe, child-friendly, and regularly maintained. We have installed proper fire safety equipment and the entire school is covered under insurance for any unforeseen eventualities, giving complete peace of mind to our parents.

Quality education is only possible with quality educators. I am proud to say that JJES is blessed with a team of dedicated and trained teachers who are not only qualified but are passionate about teaching. They undergo regular training and work with commitment to shape young minds. We believe education must prepare children for the real future. Therefore, beyond textbooks, we offer free computer classes to make every child digitally literate from an early age. We provide regular training in public speaking to build confidence and communication skills, introduce students to the basics of Indian administration and civic duties to make them responsible citizens, and teach music from its basic roots to nurture creativity and cultural values. At Jehova Jire English School - JJES, we focus on what truly matters for the future. We impart life skills, moral values, environmental awareness, and leadership qualities that will help our students succeed in life. Most importantly, we give top priority to the physical and mental well-being of every child. A healthy body and a healthy mind are essential for true learning.

I warmly invite you to visit our campus at Hall Veng, Pangbalkawn, and experience the positive and caring atmosphere we have built together. Thank you for trusting us with your child's future.

Looking forward to your frequent visit to pbkjjes.online

Mr. Timothy Ngente, JJES
Principal
