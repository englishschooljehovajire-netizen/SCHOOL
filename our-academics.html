🧒 Nursery - LKG - UKG

PRE-PRIMARY • AGE 3+ • PLAY-WAY METHOD

Studies have shown that maximum brain development of a child happens in its formative years. The Star Kids approach ensures all aspects of your child's growth is taken care of with warmth and affection. In an increasingly competitive world, it pays to have even a slight advantage. To cater to this need, we have adopted an approach that stimulates the power of thinking and assimilation. The students in response feel enthusiastic about learning and as a consequence move ahead towards success.

At JJES Pangbalkawn, our play-way method makes learning joyful through rhymes, stories, music, colours, games and activities. We focus on speaking, listening, early reading, writing, numbers, cleanliness, good manners, sharing and caring. Our safe, clean, noise-free and wheelchair-accessible campus with trained, loving teachers gives personal attention. With focus on physical and mental well-being, every child learns to speak confidently, think creatively and brighten up their future happily.

📚 Class I to Class IV

PRIMARY • FOUNDATION YEARS • SMALL CLASSES

Focus on concepts and skills and an activities-based approach forms the backbone of our teaching methodology. Emphasis is put on public speaking, interpersonal and communication skills, and team work. In these foundational years, we create a joyful, safe and child-friendly environment where learning happens through games, drawing, music and hands-on activities.

Basic literacy and numeracy are strengthened with clear pronunciation, writing practice and mental maths. Free computer classes introduce digital familiarity early. We give special care to cleanliness, punctuality, good manners and discipline. Regular interaction, small class strength and dedicated trained teachers ensure every child receives personal attention, builds confidence, curiosity and creativity. Sports, cultural programs and value education nurture physical and mental well-being, helping children to think clearly, speak boldly and brighten up their future with strong character and joyful learning at JJES Pangbalkawn.

🎓 Class V to Class VIII

UPPER PRIMARY • PREPARATION FOR HIGHER CLASSES

Every child has innate capabilities that help him/her shine. Our expert faculty provides the right guidance to bring out these latent qualities, transforming the student into a successful individual. At this crucial stage, we focus on strengthening fundamentals in Science, Maths, English and Social Studies while building logical thinking, public speaking and digital literacy through free computer classes. Regular assessments, discipline, cleanliness and moral values are given equal importance. We encourage participation in sports, music, and leadership activities to ensure balanced physical and mental growth, preparing every child to brighten up their future with confidence.

Furthermore, we introduce project-based learning, group discussions and hands-on experiments to make concepts clear and joyful. Individual attention is given to identify strengths and improve weaknesses through remedial support and mentoring. We instill punctuality, respect, environmental awareness and responsibility towards society. Our safe, clean, noise-free campus with wheelchair accessibility and trained teachers creates an ideal learning atmosphere. With fire safety, insurance coverage and inclusive education, every child feels secure, valued and motivated to explore talents, develop creativity, leadership and become a responsible citizen ready for higher studies and life challenges with confidence and integrity.
