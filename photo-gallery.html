Our Gallery: A Celebration of Learning and Togetherness

At Jehova Jire English School (JJES), we cherish every moment of our students' journey — their learning, their achievements, their smiles, and their growth. Our photo and video gallery has been created with love and appreciation, to preserve these precious memories and to celebrate the collective progress of our school community.

Purpose of Sharing Photos and Videos

The photographs and videos shared on this website are published solely for the following purposes:

1
To Appreciate and EncourageTo acknowledge the hard work, talent, and dedication of our students and teachers.
2
To Document School LifeTo preserve a record of our academic programmes, cultural events, sports, tours, celebrations, and special moments throughout the year.
3
To Promote the Growth of Our SchoolTo showcase the values, discipline, creativity, and all-round development nurtured at JJES, and to inspire future students and families.
4
To Strengthen Our School CommunityTo keep parents, guardians, and well-wishers connected with the life and progress of our school.
With Parental Consent

All photographs and videos featuring our students are shared only with the full consent and permission of their parents / guardians. We deeply respect the privacy, dignity, and safety of every child. No content is shared for personal publicity, personal promotion, or any commercial purpose.

Not for Personal Fame

This gallery is not intended to promote any individual for personal fame or recognition. Every child featured here is equally valued, and every moment shared reflects the spirit of teamwork, unity, and togetherness that defines JJES. Our focus is always on the school as a family — where every student is encouraged, every teacher is honoured, and every achievement is celebrated together.

Our Commitment to Child Safety

We are committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and positive online environment for our students. If any parent or guardian wishes to have a photograph or video of their child removed or updated, they are most welcome to contact the school administration, and we will promptly attend to the request with full respect and understanding.

A Message of Gratitude

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to our dear parents for their trust, support, and consent. Your encouragement makes it possible for us to celebrate our children and share the joy of their growth with the wider community. Together, let us continue to build a school where children learn with joy, grow with values, and shine with confidence.
