Jehova Jire English School (Pvt. Institute) is a self-managed private institution, run as its own independent entity.
During the initial years of its establishment, the school functioned smoothly without any major difficulties. However, as the year 2014 approached, the economic condition of the people of Pangbalkawn village declined significantly. As a result, JJES also faced severe challenges. At the end of that academic session, the school had to be closed.
Despite these challenges, JJES had already gained vast experience and had earned a special place in the hearts of the people. It was always remembered by the community. With strong requests and collective support from parents and well-wishers to re-establish the institution, and the school has been reformed and revived since 2025.

We will forever cherish and remember our dedicated and excellent teachers and managing members in our hearts. Some of them have even departed to their eternal heavenly abode, but as a mark of our deepest respect and gratitude, their names are listed here.
