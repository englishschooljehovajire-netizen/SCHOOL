About our School

History

Jehova Jire English School - JJES was established on 10th January 2011 by Mr. Laltlanthanga Ngente at Pangbalkawn. His vision was to uplift the very low standard of education in Pangbalkawn village. With the belief that strong foundations should begin in childhood, he started this school to provide quality education to young children. During the early days, the school was greatly supported by dedicated teachers. These included a newly married couple from Manipur, India, a young lady from Haflong, Assam, along with her children, who all contributed wholeheartedly to the growth of the school. In 2011, the school did not have its own building. Classes were conducted in a rented building owned by Mr. H. Vanlalrawna of Pangbalkawn Village. By 2012, the school had its own building and began operating from its permanent campus.
