About us

JJES Pangbalkawn refers to Jehova Jire English School, an educational institution established in 2011 located in Pangbalkawn village, within the Kolasib district of Mizoram, India. About the School Location: Pangbalkawn, Bilkhawthlir block, Kolasib district, Mizoram (PIN 796081). Estd: 10th Jan. 2011. Motto: "Brighten up your future." Activities: Regularly hosts community and academic events like health awareness programs and many mores.

Policy & Administration

At JJES, Pangbalkawn, our policy and administration is guided by our motto “Brighten Up Your Future.” Every policy we frame and every decision we make is aimed at brightening the future of our students. Our administration is built on the pillars of discipline, transparency, and child-centric values. We believe that education is not just about textbooks, but about building character, confidence, and capability. Therefore, our policies focus on holistic development - academic, moral, physical, and spiritual. We maintain a disciplined yet loving environment. Discipline at JJES is not meant to punish, but to nurture responsible citizens. Our administration ensures a safe, secure, and inspiring campus where learning becomes a joyful journey.

Management

The staff of our school are the foundation of our success. They are dedicated, hardworking, and passionate about shaping young minds for the future. With commitment and care, teachers guide students not only in academics but also in discipline, values, and character. Our administrative and support staff work tirelessly behind the scenes to ensure smooth daily operations and a safe, welcoming environment for everyone. Every member of the team brings professionalism, teamwork, and a positive attitude to the workplace. Together, the school staff inspire learning, encourage growth, and create a nurturing atmosphere where students can thrive. Their dedication and contribution are truly appreciated and play a vital role in building a brighter future for every child. We will forever cherish and remember our dedicated and excellent teachers and managing members in our hearts.
