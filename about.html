About us

JJES Pangbalkawn refers to Jehova Jire English School, an educational institution established in 2011 located in Pangbalkawn village, within the Kolasib district of Mizoram, India. About the School Location: Pangbalkawn, Bilkhawthlir block, Kolasib district, Mizoram (PIN 796081). Estd: 2011. Motto: "Brighten up your future." Activities: Regularly hosts community and academic events like health awareness programs and many mores.

Policy & Administration

At JJES, Pangbalkawn, our policy and administration is guided by our motto “Brighten Up Your Future.” Every policy we frame and every decision we make is aimed at brightening the future of our students. Our administration is built on the pillars of discipline, transparency, and child-centric values. We believe that education is not just about textbooks, but about building character, confidence, and capability. Therefore, our policies focus on holistic development - academic, moral, physical, and spiritual. We maintain a disciplined yet loving environment. Discipline at JJES is not meant to punish, but to nurture responsible citizens. Our administration ensures a safe, secure, and inspiring campus where learning becomes a joyful journey. We practice a transparent and parent-friendly system. Strong coordination between management, teachers, and parents is our strength. Our doors are always open for suggestions, because we believe in growing together. By blending traditional values with modern technology like digital learning and smart administration, we prepare our students for the challenges of tomorrow.
